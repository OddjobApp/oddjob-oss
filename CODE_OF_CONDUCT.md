# Code of conduct

This project adopts the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct, for everyone who takes part in its issues, pull
requests, discussions and other spaces.

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, and do not harass,
demean or exclude anyone. Critique the work, not the person.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **admin@oddjob.app**. Reports are read by the
maintainers and handled in confidence. The maintainers may remove comments,
commits or other contributions that break this code, and may ban a participant
temporarily or permanently, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
