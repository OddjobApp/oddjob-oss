# 🏗️ Inspect an app's scaffold

> **Capability ID:** inspect_app_scaffold
> **Capability Version:** 1.0.0
> **Manifest Schema Version:** 1
> **Category:** App Forge
> **Owner:** platform
> **Risk Profile:** Low
