## Overview

Read all scoped QuickBooks bank accounts, unpaid invoices and bills and project a 13-week scheduled cash-flow baseline. A projection of existing dated commitments, not a revenue prediction. Returns assumptions, omitted items and separate currencies. Use for our projected cashflow; do not search knowledge instead.
