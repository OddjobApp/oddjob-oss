# Published agents and forms

## What you can do
Offer a focused public experience with structured inputs.

## Example workflow
Collect announcement sign-ups or intake details through a published form; disable free-text chat for a guided flow.

## Before you start
A configured and published agent, form fields, consent, and an appropriate submission destination.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
