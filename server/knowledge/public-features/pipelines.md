# Pipelines

## What you can do
Connect jobs into reusable sequences with data passed between steps.

## Example workflow
Collect records, transform them into a summary, and route the output through a review step.

## Before you start
Configured job types, credentials, workers, and permissions for each step.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
