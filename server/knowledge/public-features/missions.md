# Durable missions

## What you can do
Carry a goal through planning, execution, pauses, and review.

## Example workflow
Prepare an operations review, pause for missing information, then resume with the recorded context.

## Before you start
A scoped mission, configured agents and tools, and any required approvals.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
