# Scribe and Media Studio

## What you can do
Move from scripted scenes to generated takes, review, and assembly.

## Example workflow
Develop a scene, compare candidate takes, and review selected material before assembling a reel.

## Before you start
Configured media providers or ComfyUI, required models and credentials, generation budget, and review gates. Provider capabilities vary.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
