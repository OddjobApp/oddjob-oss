# Organizational knowledge

## What you can do
Reuse documents, retrieved context, and mission memory.

## Example workflow
Prepare a brief from team documents and inspect the sources behind the response.

## Before you start
Accessible documents, successful extraction and indexing, and an organization provider key where AI or embeddings are required.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.

## Working with organizational knowledge
1. Add documents you are authorized to use.
2. Wait for extraction and indexing to finish; check any reported errors.
3. Ask your crew to use the relevant knowledge for a brief or mission.
4. Review the cited sources and permissions before sharing the result.

Keep reusable documents current. Mission memory and retrieved context support ongoing work, but an indexed document does not guarantee every answer will use it.
