# CREW rooms

## What you can do
Bring people and specialist agents into one shared workspace.

## Example workflow
Review a launch plan together, capture decisions, and work with forms or app surfaces beside the conversation.

## Before you start
Organization membership, permitted agent tools, and an organization-owned AI provider key.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
