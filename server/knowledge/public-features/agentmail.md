# Organization email with AgentMail

## What you can do
Connect a dedicated inbox for organizational and room communication.

## Example workflow
Keep room notifications and their replies associated with the right organization and project.

## Before you start
Each organization connects its own inbox and API key; webhook permissions and outbound approval rules still apply.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
