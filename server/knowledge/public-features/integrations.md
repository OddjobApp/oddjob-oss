# Connected services and MCP

## What you can do
Bring authorized external tools into the workspace.

## Example workflow
Use connected mail, documents, or business systems as inputs to a task, with the permissions granted to that connection.

## Before you start
Provider-specific setup, credentials or OAuth consent, enabled APIs, and the intended organization or project scope.

## Review the outcome
Check results in your workspace before using them operationally. Available actions depend on your organization permissions and configured services.
